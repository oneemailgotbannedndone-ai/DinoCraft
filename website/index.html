DinoCraft

A blocky prehistoric adventure. Tame, ride and battle dinosaurs.

Free for Windows, Mac and iPhone

Windows

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
Download
  1. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All.
  2. Open DinoCraft Launcher.exe.
  3. If Windows warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.

Mac

Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14+
Download
  1. Double-click the zip, then drag DinoCraft and DinoCraft Launcher into Applications.
  2. Open DinoCraft Launcher.
  3. If macOS blocks it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Or run xattr -cr "/Applications/DinoCraft Launcher.app" /Applications/DinoCraft.app in Terminal.

iPhone & iPad

Offline edition · iOS 16+ · sideload
Download .ipa
  1. Install AltStore or SideStore on your device.
  2. Download the .ipa on your iPhone or iPad.
  3. In AltStore/SideStore, go to My Apps, tap + and pick the file.
