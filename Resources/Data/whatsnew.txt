DinoCraft - the launcher update

Launcher
- DinoCraft now opens on a launcher: Play, Update, Cosmetics, Skin Creator, Settings and Quit.
- The Update button finds new versions by itself, downloads them and restarts into them.
- Your stats: worlds and time played. Open Game Folder shows your worlds and screenshots.

Skin Creator
- Draw your own face and shirt, pixel by pixel, with 16 colours, fill, mirror and ideas to start from.
- Save & Play goes straight into the game. Friends see your skin in multiplayer.
- Copy Code and Paste Code share skins with friends.

Cosmetics
- Hats: Explorer Hat, Cap, Crown, Top Hat, Dino Hood, Flower Crown.
- Shirt, trousers and skin colours, plus a Cape, Dino Tail or Backpack.
- Press F5 in game to see yourself from behind or from the front.

Windows now plays like the Mac
- Survival, Hardcore and Creative with creatures, crafting, furnaces, chests, farming, bows, armor, beds, villagers, portals, weather and advancements.
- Texture packs, shader packs, particles, rain and snow, and a Settings screen.

Looks
- The Mac now uses the Windows pixel font.
- Texture packs keep natural colours: TunefulCraft, Pastel Picnic, Retro Pixels and Autumn Woods.
