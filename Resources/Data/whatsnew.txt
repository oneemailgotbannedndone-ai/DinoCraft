DinoCraft - the Toonland update

Toonland
- A whole new black-and-white cartoon dimension where everything smiles.
- Build a Checker Gateway (coal + bone meal make Checker Blocks) and light it with the Ember Lighter.
- Follow the checkered roads to the stages, and listen to the Toonland song "Sunny Side Up", with words on screen.

King Grumblesaurus
- Toonland's boss: ink splatters, ground-shaking stomps and a boss health bar.
- Cheer him up to beat the game, win the Smile Trophy and hear his own theme, "Grumble Stomp".

Friends
- A new Friends page in the launcher. Press Copy My Friend Code and send it to a friend; they press Add Friend (Paste Code).
- See which friends are playing right now and join their world in one click.
- Anyone you play with shows up under "Played with recently", ready to add.
- The game tells you when a friend joins your world.

Every player is 1 of 1
- Everyone gets their own tag, like Rex#K7Q2, so no two players are the same, even with the same name.
- New players start with a one-of-a-kind look made just for them.

12 new texture packs
- TunefulCraft Remix (for our very first reviewer - thank you, Tuneful!), Candy Land, Desert Sun, Midnight, Deep Sea, Inferno,
  Pocket Green, Sketchbook, Stained Glass, Blueprint, Watercolour and Gilded. That's 19 packs to choose from.

Better tools
- Hand-drawn axes with a curved, bevelled blade and swords with a crossguard, wrapped grip and pommel.
- The launcher's Friends and Reviews buttons show who's playing and the average star rating.

Draw your whole skin
- The Skin Creator now paints every side of every body part: head (even the top), body, both arms and both legs.
- The preview turns to show the side you're painting, and "Copy Other Arm/Leg" matches your limbs in one click.

More cosmetics
- 8 new hats: Wizard Hat, Viking Helmet, Headphones, Halo, Pirate Hat, Beanie, Bunny Ears and Party Hat.
- 4 new things for your back: Angel Wings, Dragon Wings, a Jetpack and a Sword.
- More shirt, trouser, skin and accent colours.

DinoCraft Launcher
- A separate launcher program: news, updates, cosmetics, the skin creator and "How to Beat the Game". Play starts the game.

Guide and loading screens
- Press G for your next goal, step by step, from the first log to the final boss.
- New loading screens with tips and a look for every dimension.

Deeper world
- New worlds now go all the way down to Y -70: a layer of Deep Slate with caverns, lava pools and richer ores.
- Reach the bottom for the new "Rock Bottom" advancement. Older worlds keep their old floor.

Oceans, ice and swimming
- Swimming: you float at the surface, swim where you look (dive down, come back up), sprint to swim fast, sneak to sink, and jump at an edge to climb out. Your arm paddles as you swim.
- New worlds have gentler oceans and smaller icy regions. Cold seas get drifting ice floes and icebergs.

Player reviews
- The launcher's Player Reviews page shows everyone's star ratings and reviews. Pick your stars and press Write a Review.

Animations
- Items are 3D on Windows too: in your hand and on the ground. An empty hand shows your arm.
- Your hand sways when you turn, dips when you land, leans into a sprint and brings food to your mouth (with crumbs).
- Swords slash sideways; pickaxes and axes chop down.

More
- New pickaxe and bedrock textures.
- Sprinting is faster. VSync is off by default, with a Max FPS setting.
- Three new texture packs: Comic Ink, Frostbite and Neon Nights.
- Herds sometimes stampede past, and the music repeats less.
