DinoCraft - the Toonland update

Shipwrecks, crabs and buried treasure
- Shipwrecks lie in shallow seas near the coast: broken wooden hulls with a snapped mast, a captain's chest
  and a supply chest. Crabs scuttle about their decks, and on sandy beaches. They nip if you get close,
  and their claws are good to eat (cook them in a furnace).
- Shipwreck chests often hold a Treasure Map. Right-click it: it fixes on the nearest buried treasure and
  opens your map with a red X (or an arrow at the edge showing how far away it is). The treasure is buried a
  few blocks under the sand of a beach, and it's worth the dig.

Seasons, lightning fires and ocean temples
- The year now turns: spring, summer, autumn and winter, six days each. Leaves and grass go fresh green in
  spring and gold and orange in autumn. In winter snow settles on treetops and grass, and rain falls as snow.
  Crops grow fastest in spring and slowest in winter. Type /season to see which it is (or skip ahead).
- Lightning now really strikes: you'll see the bolt, it hurts anything it hits, and it can set grass, leaves
  and wood alight. Fire spreads to flammable blocks nearby and burns them away, then dies down. Rain puts it
  out, and standing in it burns. An Ember Lighter struck on the ground lights a fire too
  (/gamerule doFireTick false stops it spreading).
- Ocean Temples stand on the deep sea floor: stepped marble pyramids lit by sea lanterns, with a treasure chest
  on a gold dais inside. Each is guarded by a Mosasaurus. It circles the temple and charges at anyone who swims
  near. Beat it (it has a boss bar) for its tooth, diamonds, emeralds and plenty of experience, and the temple
  is safe from then on. Find one with /locate ocean_temple or on your map.

Amber circuits and decorations
- Amber Dust (from amber) carries power up to 15 blocks, lighting up as it goes. Power it with a Lever (flip
  it on and off), a Button (on for a second) or a Pressure Plate (on while something stands on it).
- Powered dust switches on Amber Lamps, opens doors next to it, and pushes Pistons: a piston shoves up to 12
  blocks in front of it and pulls its head back when the power stops. Pistons face you when you place them,
  or up and down when you look steeply.
- Paintings: hang one on a wall for a random picture (six to collect). Item Frames: put any item in one to
  show it off, and right-click to take it back. Armour Stands: right-click with armour to dress one up,
  or with an empty hand to take it all back. Hit it to pick the stand up.

Fossils, volcanoes, meteorites, storms and maps
- Fossil Dig Sites appear in plains, savannas and deserts: stepped pits with lantern posts, a supply chest and
  Fossil Deposits. Mine a deposit for a fossil: a Tooth, Claw, Ribs, Fern or, rarely, a Skull.
- Build a Display Case (glass over planks) and right-click it with a fossil to put it on show. Right-click it
  again to take the fossil back. Put all five kinds on display for the Curator advancement. The Fossil Hunter
  now buys fossil teeth too.
- Volcanoes rise out of the Volcanic Wastes with a lava-filled crater. Now and then they erupt: smoke, spraying
  lava and flying lava bombs that leave glowing rock where they land. Keep your distance!
- Some nights bring a meteor shower. Shooting stars streak across the sky, and a few meteorites crash down
  nearby, leaving a crater with Meteorite ore in the bottom. Mine it with an iron pickaxe or better for
  Meteorite Shards, then craft a Starmetal Pickaxe (the fastest there is) or a Starmetal Sword.
- New weather: storms. Heavier rain, darker skies, more lightning, and gusts of wind that slant the rain and
  push you about when you're out in the open.
- Paper (from 3 cattails) and a Map (8 paper around an iron ingot): right-click the map to see the land for
  about 100 blocks around you, with villages, dig sites, ruins and volcanoes marked.
- New commands: /locate dig_site, /locate volcano, /weather storm, and /event eruption or /event meteors.

Experience, enchanting and villager quests
- Defeating creatures, mining ore and taking smelted items out of a furnace now give glowing experience orbs.
  Walk near them to collect them. The green bar above your hotbar shows your level. When you die, some of it
  spills out as orbs.
- Enchanting Table (amber on top, then diamond, obsidian, diamond, then three obsidian): right-click it while
  holding a tool, weapon or piece of armour. It offers three enchantments, paid for with levels and amber:
  Efficiency, Sharpness, Unbreaking, Power, Protection and Fortune, each up to level III.
  Enchanted items shimmer, and their tooltip lists what they have.
- Villagers now ask for help: each one offers a quest a day on its trading screen, such as bringing items,
  defeating creatures or mining ore, for emeralds and experience. You can take up to three at a time.
  Your Quest Book tracks them (right-click it). Hand finished quests in to any villager.

Spears, crossbows, shields and flying Pteranodons
- Spear (flint over two sticks, diagonal): hold right-click and let go to throw it. It sticks where it lands;
  walk over it to pick it back up.
- Crossbow (sticks, iron and string): hold right-click to load an arrow, then right-click to fire a hard-hitting bolt.
- Shield (planks and iron): hold right-click to raise it. It stops attacks from the front and you move slowly.
- Tame a Pteranodon with fish, saddle it and ride it: fly where you look, sprint to go faster, jump to climb.
  Saddled Pteranodons grow big enough to carry you.

Dino eggs and babies
- Feed one of your fully healed tamed dinosaurs its favourite food and it goes looking for a partner. Do the same
  for a second one of the same kind nearby and they lay a speckled egg.
- The egg rocks harder as it gets ready and hatches after about 45 seconds into a tamed baby.
  Babies grow up over about ten minutes; feeding them makes them grow faster. They can't be ridden until grown.
- New advancements: Sky Rider, The Next Generation, Hatchling, Hold the Line, Javelin and Bolt Action.

One DinoCraft on Mac and Windows
- Joining a friend's world on Windows now runs the full game, like on the Mac: boats, fishing, taming and riding,
  the minimap, the guide, advancements, containers and everything else work when you join, not just when you host.
- Windows gets the Mac's extras: an outline around the block you're aiming at and cracks while you mine it,
  the name and health bar of whatever creature you're looking at, Tab to see who's playing, command suggestions
  in chat (Tab completes, Up/Down choose), Q to drop and 1-9 to move items while hovering a slot, Credits,
  Quit Game, Stop Hosting, Copy Invite Code and a Commands on/off switch in the pause menu, and Discord status.
- Windows settings gain Master Volume, Clouds, Interface Scale, Shader Strength, Window Size, the three graphics
  qualities, Discord options, the map and an Open Texture Packs Folder button.
- The Mac gets Windows' extras: name tags over your tamed creatures, the "Sneak to get off" hint, a Map setting
  and Unlimited in Max FPS.
- Both: the Credits are the same everywhere, and you no longer see the inside of a friend standing where you are.

Boats, fishing and a map
- Craft a Boat from 5 planks (a U shape), right-click water to put it in, right-click it to climb aboard.
  It goes where you look, sprint to row faster, sneak to get off; hit it once to pick it back up.
- Craft a Fishing Rod (3 sticks and 2 string). Cast into water, wait for the float to dip under with a splash,
  then reel in quickly: mostly fish, some junk, and now and then treasure (luckier at night and in the rain).
- String drops from Cave Crawlers and Sand Scorpions, or craft 3 from any wool.
- A minimap in the top-right corner, coloured from the real blocks, with your pets, friends and a red X where
  you last died. Press M for a big map or to hide it (change the key in Controls). Underground it maps the cave.
- New advancements: Ahoy!, Gone Fishing and Sunken Treasure.
- Fixed thin cracks that showed across big stretches of water.

Taming, riding and pets
- Tame creatures by feeding them their favourite food (it can take a few tries):
  Trikey, Parasaurolophus, Iguanodon, Stegosaurus, Longneck, Pachycephalosaurus and Gallimimus eat berries or wheat;
  Raptors and Compys eat raw dino meat; Dodos, Oviraptors and Pookpooks make lovely pets.
- Tamed creatures follow you, catch up if you run off, and sit when you right-click them.
- Craft a Saddle (5 dino hide + an iron ingot), put it on a big plant-eater and ride it: steer with your movement keys,
  jump with Jump, sprint to gallop and sneak to get off. Gallimimus is the fastest.
- Tamed Raptors and Compys are guards: they fight whatever attacks you and whatever you attack.
- Name them with /name Blue while looking at them. Feed them to heal them. They're saved with your world.
- New advancements: Best Friends, Dino Rider and Clever Girls.

New dinosaurs
- Pachycephalosaurus: a dome-headed headbutter on the plains and mountains (leave it alone and it leaves you alone).
- Gallimimus: an ostrich-like sprinter that races off when startled.
- Iguanodon: a big, gentle forest plant-eater with spiked thumbs.
- Oviraptor: a small, bright feathered forager with a tall crest.
- Microraptor: a four-winged glider flitting through forests and jungles.
- Therizinosaurus: a towering feathered giant with huge claws, deep in the jungle. Don't make it angry.
- Each has its own spawn egg.

Better villagers
- Every villager has a face now, with eyes, brows and a big nose, and stands with folded arms.
- Each profession dresses the part: Farmers in straw hats, Toolsmiths with goggles and a leather apron,
  Masons in caps and white aprons, Fossil Hunters in explorer's helmets with a satchel and neckerchief.

Better textures
- Wood: planks with wavy grain, knots, lit edges and nail heads; bark with deep furrows; log ends with
  growth rings, a drying crack and a rim of bark (oak, redwood and palm).
- Dirt with crumbly clods, pebbles and roots; grass with little blades, and grass edges that drip down the side.
- Sand with wind ripples; stone bricks as big bevelled blocks (plain, mossy and cracked); crisper clay bricks.
- All 19 texture packs are updated to match.

Fixes and controls
- Fixed a crash while exploring cold seas: an iceberg next to land or a shallow bank crashed the game.
  It happened more often with a big render distance, since more of the world is being built.
- Updates: the launcher now tells you whether the last update worked. On Mac, if macOS blocks the launcher from
  replacing the apps, nothing is left half-copied: the new apps' folder opens so you can drag them into Applications,
  or allow DinoCraft Launcher in System Settings > Privacy & Security > App Management.
- Windows: change your keys in Settings > Controls. Click an action, then press a key or mouse button.
  Joined games use your keys too.
- A better hunger bar: pixel drumsticks with a gold rim while you're still full, a shake when you're starving,
  and a ripple when you eat.

Life under the sea
- Fish: schools of Cod and Salmon, and Clownfish and Blue Tang on the reefs. They swim all around you,
  dart away when you hit them and flop about on land. Catch them for Raw Fish and Tropical Fish; cook fish in a furnace.
- Coral reefs in warm, shallow seas: mounds of coral in five colours, branching coral and glowing Sea Lanterns.
- Kelp forests sway in cooler seas, and seagrass meadows cover the sea floor. Smelt kelp into Dried Kelp to snack on.
- Under water now really looks like under water on Windows too: blue-green fog all around (a little clearer on Mac as well).
- Craft Sea Lanterns from coral, glass and a torch. New advancements: Just Keep Swimming, Something Fishy and Under the Sea.

Toonland
- A whole new dimension of golden meadows, birch woods and glowing sunblooms.
- Build a Checker Gateway (coal + bone meal make Checker Blocks) and light it with the Ember Lighter.
- Follow the checkered roads to the stages, and listen to the Toonland song "Sunny Side Up", with words on screen.

King Grumblesaurus
- Toonland's boss: mud splatters, ground-shaking stomps and a boss health bar.
- Cheer him up to beat the game, win the Smile Trophy and hear his own theme, "Grumble Stomp".

Global leaderboard
- A new Leaderboard in the launcher: see everyone's play time, kills, blocks mined and placed, distance, crafting, deaths and bosses.
- Your stats are counted in every world and shared automatically every few minutes while you play.

Better ores
- Every ore is repainted: glossy coal lumps, rusty iron and shiny gold nuggets, faceted diamonds,
  hexagonal emerald crystals and glowing amber, each set into the stone with a shadow and a glint.

Bigger caves
- New worlds have wider tunnels and much bigger caverns, and the deep slate caves are bigger too.

Recipe book on Windows
- The inventory and crafting bench now have a Recipe Book: see every recipe, and click one to fill the grid.

Fixes
- Mac: joining a friend no longer gives up straight away while macOS asks for Local Network permission.
- If DinoCraft ever crashes, the launcher offers to send the crash report so it can be fixed (now on Windows too,
  with the exact error and where it happened).
- Windows: DinoCraft gets 8 times more room for its main thread, which can stop some crashes.

A crafting-table look
- Menus, inventories and chests are now oak planks in a dark wooden frame with iron nails, on Mac and Windows.

Zoom
- Hold Z to zoom in. Scroll while holding it to zoom further in or out. Let go and it zooms back out.

Double chests
- Put two chests side by side (facing the same way) and they join into one Large Chest with twice the room.

Multiplayer
- /msg <player> <message> sends a private message (also /tell and /w).
- Hardcore worlds in multiplayer: players who die can only spectate, even if they leave and rejoin.
- Hardcore hearts look different: dark crimson with a gold crest.

Toonland looks like DinoCraft now
- No more black-and-white cartoon filter: golden meadows, birch woods, limestone, marble checker roads and glowing sunblooms.
- King Grumblesaurus is a real bronze rex with a gold crown.

More
- Render distance goes up to 64 chunks (for strong computers).
- Axes, swords, shovels and hoes all match the new pickaxe style.

Friends
- A new Friends page in the launcher. Press Copy My Friend Code and send it to a friend; they press Add Friend (Paste Code).
- See which friends are playing right now and join their world in one click.
- Anyone you play with shows up under "Played with recently", ready to add.
- The game tells you when a friend joins your world.

Every player is 1 of 1
- Everyone gets their own tag, like Rex#K7Q2, so no two players are the same, even with the same name.
- New players start with a one-of-a-kind look made just for them.

12 new texture packs
- TunefulCraft Remix (for our very first reviewer - thank you, Tuneful!), Candy Land, Desert Sun, Midnight, Deep Sea, Inferno,
  Pocket Green, Sketchbook, Stained Glass, Blueprint, Watercolour and Gilded. That's 19 packs to choose from.

Better tools
- Hand-drawn axes with a curved, bevelled blade and swords with a crossguard, wrapped grip and pommel.
- The launcher's Friends and Reviews buttons show who's playing and the average star rating.

Draw your whole skin
- The Skin Creator now paints every side of every body part: head (even the top), body, both arms and both legs.
- The preview turns to show the side you're painting, and "Copy Other Arm/Leg" matches your limbs in one click.

More cosmetics
- 8 new hats: Wizard Hat, Viking Helmet, Headphones, Halo, Pirate Hat, Beanie, Bunny Ears and Party Hat.
- 4 new things for your back: Angel Wings, Dragon Wings, a Jetpack and a Sword.
- More shirt, trouser, skin and accent colours.

DinoCraft Launcher
- A separate launcher program: news, updates, cosmetics, the skin creator and "How to Beat the Game". Play starts the game.

Guide and loading screens
- Press G for your next goal, step by step, from the first log to the final boss.
- New loading screens with tips and a look for every dimension.

Deeper world
- New worlds now go all the way down to Y -70: a layer of Deep Slate with caverns, lava pools and richer ores.
- Reach the bottom for the new "Rock Bottom" advancement. Older worlds keep their old floor.

Oceans, ice and swimming
- Swimming: you float at the surface, swim where you look (dive down, come back up), sprint to swim fast, sneak to sink, and jump at an edge to climb out. Your arm paddles as you swim.
- New worlds have gentler oceans and smaller icy regions. Cold seas get drifting ice floes and icebergs.

Player reviews
- The launcher's Player Reviews page shows everyone's star ratings and reviews. Pick your stars and press Write a Review.

Animations
- Items are 3D on Windows too: in your hand and on the ground. An empty hand shows your arm.
- Your hand sways when you turn, dips when you land, leans into a sprint and brings food to your mouth (with crumbs).
- Swords slash sideways; pickaxes and axes chop down.

More
- New pickaxe and bedrock textures.
- Sprinting is faster. VSync is off by default, with a Max FPS setting.
- Three new texture packs: Comic Ink, Frostbite and Neon Nights.
- Herds sometimes stampede past, and the music repeats less.
